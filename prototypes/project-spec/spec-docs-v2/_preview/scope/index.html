Project Spec 2.0 · Overview

Every feature group in the 2024 agreement, against what v2.0 does today, with per-scope status counts.

Exhibit A splits the work into four scopes (WEB, APP, ADMIN, OPS) and eight phases. Phase 6 is Product Launch (Exhibit A, p5; Gantt p6). These pages follow the agreement’s own story: Play with Relationship Aspects, Register, Invite Counterparty, Give Opinions, Share & Discuss (p4). Each page sets what was agreed against what v2.0 does today.

Confidential: this page draws on the 2024 Consulting Agreement, which is under NDA (Exhibit C). It stays local and off the public preview. Fees are not reproduced; see Exhibit B.
