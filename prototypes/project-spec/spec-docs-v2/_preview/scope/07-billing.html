v2.0 Today

Payment tab while the 7 days run · Bundle 08

Stage 6 paused: nothing is deleted · Bundle 03

ID What v2.0 does
APP-42 7 days free, starting when the other person accepts, then one free Extra Week before Stage 6 pauses. No card at sign-up.
APP-43 One plan, Basic, monthly, for one relationship: a PM pricing decision (Family removed by PR #1237, Single Annual by PR #1241). Settled scope.
APP-44 Stripe checkout and signed webhook; Payment tab.
APP-45 “{initiator} covers this relationship.” Counterparty pages carry no billing words.
APP-46 Payment tab shows plan, trial state and after-trial price. Invoice access was not evidenced.
APP-47 Not built. The billing schema leaves coupons to the provider.
APP-48 When the trial and Extra Week end, Stage 6 pauses read-only and nothing is deleted; Add a Card resumes it.
