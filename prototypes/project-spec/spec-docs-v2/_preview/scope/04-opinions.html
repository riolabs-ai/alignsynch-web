v2.0 Today

Answer the Assessment · Bundle 10

ID What v2.0 does
APP-26 Answer the Assessment: one Aspect at a time, five steps labelled by its two Values, saves as you go.
APP-27 “Private until you both finish”; answers sealed for Round 1.
APP-28 Aspects Are Locked; “Changes are proposed and agreed by both of you.”
APP-29 Six stages people can act on: Details, Settings, Aspects, Account, Invite, Align. The 1–4 relationship ladder (Invited, Connected, Sharing, Aligned) is kept in the model.

Status

ID Requirement Status Acceptance (MVP)
APP-26 Each person gives Feel and Wish (Basic) or also Think and Guess (Advanced) per Aspect, on a 1–5 scale between its Left and Right Values. ✓ Delivered The answer screen asks exactly the mode’s opinions per Aspect and saves 1–5.
APP-27 Answers stay private until both people have finished. ✓ Delivered Neither person can read the other’s answers before both submit.
APP-28 Once invited, the Aspects are locked; changes go through a proposal the other person approves. ✓ Delivered After the invite, Aspects can’t be edited directly; a proposal flow is offered.
APP-29 A Stage Bar shows eight stages (A–D user, 1–4 relationship). ⇄ Offset n/a: replaced.
