Every v2 design and feature, who it's for, what it gives them, its status and its best screenshot, with gaps flagged.

Shot tiers, in priority order: B = Bundle reference render · Q = QA capture of the built app · S = spec or repo asset · P = prototype render · — = none. Design status: Designed (bundle) · Prototype Only · Planned (no screen) · Removed. App: the PR that shipped it, or confirm when it isn’t verified. Bundle paths are short for spec-design-v2-bundles/NN-*/reference/. P shots taken 18 Nov are on the box at /workspace/alignsynch/out/new-surfaces-shots/.
