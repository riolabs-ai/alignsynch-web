Feel vs. Wish: The Two Questions Behind Every Gap

Product · AlignSynch Team · placeholder date · 4 min read

Most check-ins ask one question: how are things? AlignSynch asks two, for every Aspect of a relationship. How is it now, and how do you wish it were?

Feel Is Today

Feel is your honest read of the present. On Contact Frequency, a 2 might mean you're in touch less than you'd like.

Wish Is Where You'd Like It to Go

Wish is the direction. If your Feel is a 2 and your Wish is a 4, you've said something important without having to say it out loud.

Telling someone that something needs to change is tricky and difficult.

Where the Gap Shows Up

When you've both answered, you see both Feels and both Wishes side by side. The Gap is the distance between you, and it's the place to start talking.
