About

Talking for Hours Isn't the Same as Agreeing.

AlignSynch exists to give two people a soft, simple way to find out if they're on the same page.

Where It Came From

A Tool Built for Real Work First.

LOOP Feedback

The concept was developed by Walt Johnson to manage the risk of not being in alignment with counterparties. Early versions ran on SharePoint for manager–staff and customer–vendor relationships, under the name LOOP Feedback (Lots Of Objective Parameters).

2020 · Version 1

A first web version covered Feel and Wish. It couldn't take payments, send email or host a chat.

Today · 2.0

Six stages, private answers, a side-by-side Align view, Discussion and Chat, for any two people.

What We Believe

Soft, Not Sharp

Hard topics deserve a gentle way in.

Mutual, Not Monitored

Two people working on it together, not a company watching.

Private Until Ready

No one sees your answers until you've both finished.

A Tune-Up

For tweaking and optimizing. Not for emergencies.
