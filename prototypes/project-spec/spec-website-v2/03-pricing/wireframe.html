Billing Questions

Who pays?

Only the person who starts the relationship. The person you invite joins free and never sees a price, a trial or a card.

When does the free week start?

When the other person accepts. Setting up and waiting are free and don't use any days.

Do I need a card to start?

No. You can add one any time during your 7 days.

What happens if I don't add a card?

Stage 6 pauses. Everything is saved and read-only. Add a card or take one free Extra Week to resume.

Can I have more than one relationship?

Each relationship is its own Basic plan. Plans for groups come later.
